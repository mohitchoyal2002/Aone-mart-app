# Product image library

These photographs were downloaded from the free Unsplash collection under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The original page and image
URL for each asset are recorded in `sources.json`. No Unsplash+ assets are used.
The bundled files are resized/compressed for the app; they are not sold as a
standalone photo collection.

Photos illustrate product types, not a particular brand or package. Product
detail pages identify automatic photos as representative images. Store-provided
product photos take priority. Imports do not require an image column.

The app loads local thumbnails in lists and local 1024px photos in details.
There are no live photo searches or third-party requests when browsing.
